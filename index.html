Chasing the decisive moment, one street at a time.

I wander cities with a camera, searching for the raw poetry that lives between heartbeats: a shadow cast just right, a stranger's glance, the geometry of urban chaos resolving into something quietly beautiful.

My work is rooted in the street photography tradition: no staging, no retouching, no waiting for the light to be perfect. The imperfection is the point. The grain is the truth.

“Your first 10,000 photographs are your worst.” — Henri Cartier-Bresson
I'm well past that. The next 10,000 are where it gets interesting.
